## 🚀 Featured Work

### 🏢 Dawood Designers Employee Portal

A full-scale employee management platform covering:

- Employee records
- Attendance & shift management
- Payroll
- Reporting
- Permissions / RBAC
- Documents
- Multi-company support
- Data-management workflows
- Group chat
- Employee letters & approvals
- Gemini-powered employee assistant

The system also includes a **Python ZKTeco K40 bridge** that synchronizes biometric attendance data with Firebase.

### 🤖 Gemini Employee Assistant

Integrated the **Gemini API** into the employee portal to provide an AI assistant with **permission-scoped access to an employee's own portal data**, allowing employees to ask questions and receive personalized assistance.

### 🐍 ZKTeco Attendance Bridge

A Python service connecting a **ZKTeco K40 biometric device** to Firebase, automating fingerprint-based check-in/check-out and enabling real-time attendance reporting.
